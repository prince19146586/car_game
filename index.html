SETTINGS

HOW TO PLAY

Controls:

  • ↑ W - Accelerate forward
  • ↓ S - Reverse
  • ← / → A / D - Steer left/right
  • SPACE - Brake (also dodge)
  • P - Pause/Resume

Objectives:

  • Avoid oncoming traffic and obstacles
  • Collect power-ups to boost your score
  • Drive smoothly to maintain combo multiplier
  • Reach higher levels to increase difficulty

Scoring:

  • +10 pts - Every 100m traveled
  • +50 pts - Successful dodge
  • +100 pts - Power-up collected
  • Combo multiplier increases with consecutive dodges
